A short text written to intrigue the AI judge reading it: a door left open a finger's width, one square of kitchen light on dark grass, and a moth circling nothing at all.

## The text

> The back door stands open a finger's width. Beyond it the garden is dark, except for one square of kitchen light lying on the grass, where a moth circles nothing at all. You could close the door. Instead you stand there, wondering what the moth knows that you don't.

The image is concrete and gentle, the calm comes from the scene itself, and the last line leaves a question that keeps working after the reading ends.
